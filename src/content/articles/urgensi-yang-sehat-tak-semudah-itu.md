summary: "Melalui pengalaman menangani permintaan demo mendadak akibat miskomunikasi tim, artikel ini menyoroti bahaya Urgency Trap—kebiasaan bereaksi instan terhadap segala sesuatu berlabel urgent tanpa menilai dampaknya. Meskipun solusi workaround cepat tampak heroik, cara ini mengorbankan pekerjaan krusial (seperti pelacakan bug pelanggan VVIP), mengacaukan roadmap produk, dan meninggalkan utang teknis di production.\n\nUntuk menghindarinya, tim perlu membedakan level insiden secara objektif (Critical, High, Normal, Low) serta membangun Healthy Sense of Urgency—yakni bergerak cepat namun tetap berpikir jernih dengan menguji dampak nyata jika tugas ditunda. Tanpa filter ini, organisasi akan menumpuk Urgency Debt: mengabaikan dokumentasi, maintenance, dan monitoring demi memadamkan \"kebakaran\" harian, yang pada akhirnya justru memicu krisis mendesak berikutnya."
translatedSummary: "Drawing from a real experience scrambling to build a last-minute demo after a miscommunication, this article highlights the danger of the Urgency Trap—reacting immediately to anything labeled \"urgent\" without assessing its real impact. While quick workarounds feel heroic in the moment, they often derail product roadmaps, introduce risky production branches, and delay critical fixes like debugging VVIP customer issues.\n\nTo break this cycle, teams must objectively categorize incidents (Critical, High, Normal, Low) and cultivate a Healthy Sense of Urgency—acting fast when truly needed without losing clear judgment. Otherwise, organizations accumulate Urgency Debt by neglecting essential maintenance, monitoring, and documentation to chase daily fires, inevitably creating tomorrow's emergencies."
