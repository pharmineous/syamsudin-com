summary: "Data penting dalam reporting bisa tiba-tiba kosong bukan karena ada yang lalai, melainkan karena setiap peran—developer, bisnis, analis, Data Ops, dan DB Architect—bekerja sesuai tugasnya masing-masing tanpa menjaga \"kontrak\" data bersama. Akar masalahnya adalah data masih dianggap sekadar implementasi teknis, padahal seharusnya diperlakukan sebagai produk.\n\nSolusinya bersifat organisasional melalui empat langkah: Data Contract (kejelasan owner dan notifikasi perubahan), Layered Tracking (pemisahan level Eksperimental, Operasional, dan Governed), Deprecation Window (masa transisi 1–3 bulan sebelum field dihapus), serta Tracking Review Board (sinkronisasi rutin 30 menit per bulan). Pada akhirnya, kunci utamanya adalah desain komunikasi lintas tim agar semua perspektif berjalan selaras."
translatedSummary: "Critical reporting data can suddenly go blank not because someone failed, but because every role—developers, business teams, analysts, Data Ops, and DB Architects—did their own job without maintaining a shared data contract. The root cause is treating data merely as a technical implementation rather than a product.\n\nThe solution is organizational, built on four practices: Data Contracts (clear field owners and change notifications), Layered Tracking (separating Experimental, Operational, and Governed tiers), a Deprecation Window (1–3 months' notice before removing fields), and a monthly 30-minute Tracking Review Board. Ultimately, success comes down to communication design that harmonizes every team's perspective."
